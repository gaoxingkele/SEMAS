# Exact Four-Pillar Boundary and Lin Fan Fixture

The default calendar provider must not turn an unavailable exact backend into a
plausible but different chart. `auto` now requires `lunar_python` or `sxtwl`;
the approximate provider remains explicit and blocks Hengmen analysis. [source:
lunar_python 1.4.8]

For Lin Fan, born 1978-04-14 06:50 in Sanming, the verified regression pillars
are `WuWu / BingChen / BingWu / XinMao`. This chart is a test fixture for
calendar provenance, not evidence that a symbolic method predicts life events.
[source: lunar_python 1.4.8]

The factual fixture includes 13 annual events from 1996 through 2020, including
positive, negative, and mixed outcomes. Initial scoring can test whether an
event theme activates, but cannot yet evaluate event direction because the
existing event taxonomy joins promotion and promotion failure, and business
gain and investment loss. [source: user-provided Lin Fan event timeline,
2026-07-14]
