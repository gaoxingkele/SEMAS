# Exact Pillars Precede Rule Quality

A detailed Hengmen rule engine cannot recover from a changed month command or
day master. Treating a civil-calendar approximation as an automatic fallback
creates false certainty because every later rule receives internally coherent
but incorrect premises. The correct behavior is to require an exact calendar
backend or block the method. [source: lunar_python 1.4.8]

The Lin Fan timeline also separates occurrence from direction. A year can have
a strong career or wealth activation while the observed result is a failed
promotion or investment loss. A scorer that only asks whether a career/wealth
topic appeared must not be described as predicting success. [source:
user-provided Lin Fan event timeline, 2026-07-14]
