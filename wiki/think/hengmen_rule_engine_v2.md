# Rule Evidence Must Not Be Replaced By Score Presence

The previous Hengmen scorer treated the presence of generic analysis fields as
evidence. The revised design asks concrete questions: which pattern is proposed,
what breaks it, what rescues it, and whether branch relations support or damage
the required force. [source: `tools/格局横门断.docx`]

Monthly evidence and counterexamples need explicit source rows. Selecting a
favourable month after an event, or calling a sourced positive event a complete
validation without non-event years, would turn calibration into leakage.

The structural topics need asymmetric treatment. A storage clash can be logged
as a small activation condition because it has a concrete chart relation, but
it cannot be converted into an outcome. Combination direction and a partial
trine carry more interpretive ambiguity, so the engine exposes them as evidence
objects with low confidence rather than score bonuses. [source: `tools/格局横门断.docx`]

Candidate labels are part of the method, not presentation detail. Yang Blade
shares some peer-family mechanics but is separately named in the source rule
table, so the engine must preserve that distinction and reveal its coverage
receipt to downstream AHP consumers. [source: `tools/格局横门断.docx`]

An AHP label is a contract. Reusing an annual score for major-luck support, or
placing a monthly score under an annual label, can produce a plausible total
while destroying the evidence trail. The receipt now exposes each timing layer
and normalizes only the vocabulary boundary between precise ten gods and event
themes. [source: `tools/格局横门断.docx`]

Candidate conditions must separate intrinsic chart identity from external
configuration. Counting the day master as evidence that a peer-family pattern
is overrun by peers is circular. The repaired logic evaluates only the three
other visible stems for support, failure, and rescue. [source: `tools/格局横门断.docx`]

Role families are too coarse for the book's rescue logic. A candidate can have
the same broad wealth, expression, and authority themes while changing from
support to failure based on whether the expression is food-god or
hurting-officer, and whether killing is exposed. Those distinctions belong in
the candidate receipt before AHP aggregation. [source: `tools/格局横门断.docx`]

Timing evidence also needs compatibility, not presence. An active major-luck
period can support, damage, or mix with a selected pattern; treating every
period as a bonus erases the very pattern-specific logic that the timing layer
is meant to test. [source: `tools/格局横门断.docx`]

Relation evidence has an ownership boundary. A clash between two unrelated
branches should not be charged to every candidate, and a combination must say
what it brings to the relevant root before it can be called affectionate. The
engine therefore emits directional evidence instead of a raw relation count.
[source: `tools/格局横门断.docx`]

Virtual structures are evidence-strength problems, not binary transformations.
Repetition can justify more attention, but it cannot replace the exposure and
independent-event constraints needed for a candidate to enter scoring. [source:
`tools/格局横门断.docx`]

Independent agents need independent receipts. A named weight without its own
evidence payload invites hidden coupling and makes disagreements impossible to
inspect. The AHP result now preserves the separate reasoning artifacts that
produced its score. [source: `tools/格局横门断.docx`]

An AHP weight vector is not enough for an auditable architecture. The output
must state its criterion order, pairwise relation, and whether that relation is
method-derived or evidence-fitted. The Hengmen matrix is source-priority
derived, so current event outcomes cannot silently alter it. [source:
`tools/格局横门断.docx`]

Coverage must be a first-class artifact. Marking a symbolic rule executable is
not the same as proving an outcome claim, and case-order or analogy rules can
remain partial without being silently treated as implemented. The catalog makes
those boundaries inspectable. [source: `tools/格局横门断.docx`]

Observable ordering facts should be preserved before their interpretation is
automated. A visible finance/resource separation is useful evidence for an
ordering-sensitive candidate, but it cannot by itself prove the stronger claim
that the two forces do not interfere. [source: `tools/格局横门断.docx`]

Negative evidence is only meaningful when it is bound to the claimed year. A
matching number of annual rows cannot stand in for year identity; otherwise a
calibration can invent falsification from unrelated timing evidence. [source:
`tools/格局横门断.docx`]

Temporal precision has its own ambiguity. When an explicit month conflicts with
an ISO date, selecting one silently is a data decision disguised as a timing
rule. The correct engine behavior is a neutral receipt until the calendar basis
is resolved. [source: `tools/格局横门断.docx`]
