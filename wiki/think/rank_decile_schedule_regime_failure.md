# Rank-Decile Schedule Regime Failure

An evolved policy may correctly resolve to the identity transform. Here,
absolute current-rank staircases improved the development period but reversed
in the later regime. The first dynamic winner's blind Sharpe was -0.5719 while
the static baseline reached 0.3643 [source: local first-run receipt].

The legacy trim then looked strong in the short 2026 final segment but had
Sharpe -0.6092 in the 2024-2025 audit segment. Selecting it after opening 2026
would turn a verifier into another optimizer [source: local recovery receipt].

The methodological change is to preserve identity schedules as first-class
genomes and rollback targets. A future search should test a genuinely different
state variable, such as rank-change velocity or volatility regime, under a new
holdout rather than adding complexity to the failed absolute-rank surface.
